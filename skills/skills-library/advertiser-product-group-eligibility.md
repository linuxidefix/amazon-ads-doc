## Advertiser Product Group Eligibility Skill

**What does this skill do?**
This skill guides agents through creating and querying advertiser product group eligibility requests for ad product groups like Amazon DSP (ADSP). It covers the request lifecycle from submission through approval or rejection, including duplicate prevention and rejection reason handling.

**When to read this skill:**
Read this skill when your agent needs to check whether an advertiser has applied for ADSP access, submit a new eligibility request, or understand why a previous request was rejected.

* `advertiser_product_group_eligibility-create`
* `advertiser_product_group_eligibility-query`
