## Product Eligibility Skill

**What does this skill do?**
This skill lets agents check per-`ASIN` advertising eligibility for a given ad type (Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, or DSP). It answers the question "Can these specific products be advertised?" and surfaces reason codes for ineligible products such as out-of-stock, restricted category, or policy violations.

**When to read this skill:**
Read this skill when your agent needs to verify whether specific `ASIN`s can be advertised before creating campaigns or ad groups targeting those products.

* `eligibility-product_list`
