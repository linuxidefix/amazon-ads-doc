## Advertiser Accounts Skill

**What does this skill do?**
This skill guides agents through creating, querying, and updating advertiser accounts (global accounts) via the `account_management` APIs. It covers the full account lifecycle—from registration through approval—and includes rules for mutable vs. immutable fields, selling account linking, and manager account association for DSP enablement.

**When to read this skill:**
Read this skill when your agent needs to register a new advertiser account, look up existing accounts by ID or filter, or update account details such as display name, industry, or business information.

* `account_management-create_advertiser_account`
* `account_management-query_advertiser_account`
* `account_management-update_advertiser_account`
